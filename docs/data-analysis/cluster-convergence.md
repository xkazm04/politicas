## F19 — ODS's fiscal divergence is coherent and directional

Of **271** fiscal-budget votes, ODS-the-club diverges from its bloc (B) on just **16
(5.9%)** — but the divergence is *perfectly consistent*: on **all 16, ODS votes NO while
bloc B votes YES, and on all 16 it crosses to bloc A (ANO)**. Concentrated on the
**sales-records / evidence tržeb (EET) bill (8)** and the **state budget (7)**.

So ODS's break from its coalition bloc ([[cluster-theme-rebellion]] F16, [[cluster-blocs]] P3)
is not scatter — it is a **coherent, more-fiscally-restrictive position**: ODS opposes
specific tax-administration and budget measures the rest of bloc B backs, siding with the
ANO majority to reject them. A real ideological seam inside the coalition bloc.

## F20 — independence and bridging are DISTINCT roles

Correlation between an MP's `contested_rebellion_score` (breaking their club line on
contested themes) and their **cross-bloc co-voting agreement** (bridging to the other bloc):
**Pearson = 0.081** — essentially zero. The top independents (Babka 26.6, Pikora 20.4,
Bureš 20.0) sit near the median cross-bloc agreement (0.41), not at the top; the top
cross-bloc co-voters (Plaga, Babiš 0.51–0.52) have *low* independence. Only **Haas (ODS)**
is both (score 21, cross-agree 0.50).

Two takeaways: (1) *rebelling against your own club* and *reaching across the aisle* are
**different behaviours** — a rebel usually breaks idiosyncratically, not toward the other
bloc. (2) **True bridges barely exist** — even the most cross-voting MP tops out at 0.52
agreement with the other bloc (vs 0.9+ within), underscoring the polarization.

## F21 — the consensus windows are housekeeping, not policy

The two windows where blocs agreed ([[cluster-agenda-control]]):

  + 1 fiscal — chamber *self-constitution* (electing officers, forming committees), before

- **June 2026** (agreed share 0.51): **138 state-honours** + 20 procedure + 11 appointments,
  with only trace substantive agreement (housing 10, civil-service 10). The "blip" is the
  **honours season**, not a policy thaw.

**Confirms [[cluster-bloc-theme]] P10**: consensus is only ever on the low-contestedness
themes (procedure-setup, honours, appointments). Genuine cross-bloc *policy* agreement does
not occur in this chamber — the apparent consensus windows are ceremonial and organizational.
