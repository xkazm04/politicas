# Batch 009 — Q-effort-16 paid off: the 29 sample-scoped self-references (2026-07-27)

*Case ② Effort · solo mode · population unchanged (207/207) — a scoped prose-quality pass, the
batch-007 operation at 1/7th scale with batch-008's hardened mechanics.*

Batch 008's verifier-earned jargon rule (sample-scoped self-reference: „v tomto přehledu",
„v této skupině", „ze svého vzorku", superlatives over the analyst's own working set) withheld a
measured **29 field-instances on 28 person nodes** (batches 001–007 prose) at render. This batch
rewrites them.

## Mechanics

- **Inputs**: `extract-q16-inputs.ts` — full text + exact matched substrings + the role stats
  needed to restate a comparative absolutely. Re-measured at extraction: 28 nodes / 29 instances,
  and nothing else in the corpus trips any rule.
- **Army**: 2 Sonnet groups × 14. Preference order: restate absolutely from the unit's own data,
  else DE-SCOPE (drop the sample reference, keep the plain fact). Outcome: 27 de-scoped, 1
  restated (Flek — the claim was self-referential to his own committee list, not sample-scoped);
  the agents rightly refused absolute restatement everywhere a comparative would need
  full-population data they didn't have.
- **Minimal-diff PROVEN in merge** (`merge-batch-009.ts`): corrected text must trip zero rules,
  every non-offending original sentence must survive verbatim (the money-safety proof — a money or
  hedge sentence physically cannot have changed, which is why this pass needs no fresh P51 money
  re-verification), new-sentence count bounded by offending-sentence count, length growth ≤250
  chars. **28/28 passed, 0 drops.**
- **Gate**: 28/28 PASS. 3 soft warnings → 2 pre-existing subset framings (Nerušil, Mádlová —
  out of scope, untouched by the diff-proof) + 1 real find: Výborný's offending sentence carried a
  STALE count („tři doložené tisky, 2+1") contradicted by the pass-34 data (6 = 2 first + 4 co) —
  corrected by the driver since the sentence was already open for rewrite, numbers taken from the
  graph.
- **Persisted pass 38** (props-merge, 28 nodes). Re-measured on a fresh copy against the real
  `jargonViolationDetails` import: **0 withheld field-instances across all 207** — the full dossier
  corpus renders clean for the first time since the rule landed.

## Lesson

A verifier-earned rule that withholds honest-but-unshippable prose creates its own debt on
arrival — pinning the count at rule-landing time (29) made this pass a bounded, one-session
operation instead of an open-ended "clean the corpus" chore. The minimal-diff proof (non-offending
sentences byte-preserved) is the cheapest two-layer substitute yet: it converts the P51
"re-verify everything the rewrite touched" burden into "prove the rewrite touched nothing else".
