# UX audit — Politicas, 2026-07-27

**Method.** Walked the public surfaces as a Czech citizen with a real question, over the live dev server at
`localhost:3000`, then read the components and loaders behind what rendered. Every criticism below points at a
file, a line, or a string that actually shipped to the browser. `/admin` not judged (no token). Forensic *content*
in `features/lawwatch/` deliberately not judged — only structure, navigation, scale.

**Headline.** The operator's verdict is right, but the diagnosis is too kind. This is not "a mess of promising use
cases." Three surfaces (`/zebricek`, `/poslanec/[id]`, `/penize/[id]`) contain genuinely good work — the MP
dossiers are the best civic-data prose I have read in a hobby project. They are surrounded by, and outranked by,
a demo layer that is **fabricated**, and by pipeline chrome that the loops were rewarded for producing. The app
does not have a truthfulness problem. It has an **editing** problem: nothing on any page is ranked by how much a
reader learns, and several things are ranked by a number that the app's own explainer tells you not to read.

---

## Part 0 — The finding that reframes everything else

**The first two screens of the product are fiction, and they are stamped with real sources.**

`lib/civic/data.ts` — whose own header comment reads *"Všechna čísla jsou ilustrativní mock…"* — is the sole data
source for `app/page.tsx` (landing) and `app/dashboard/page.tsx`. Neither is `async`. Neither touches the
database. What a first-time visitor sees:

- **Petra Nováková, Piráti, Praha 2, 88,3** (`lib/civic/data.ts:66`) — does not exist.
- **Eva Pokorná / Martin Dvořák / Karel Hruška / Tomáš Beran** (L79, L92, L105, L117) — do not exist.
- **Silnice MSK a.s., IČO 258 41 991, 4,2 mil. Kč** (L242) and **Agrofond s.r.o., IČO 470 12 336** (L243) — do not
  exist. Note that these are rendered *with fabricated IČO numbers*, on a site whose entire methodology section
  explains that the eight-digit IČO is the trustworthy join key.
- A live activity feed of invented events: *"dnes 09:14 Nová smlouva 4,2 mil. Kč — Silnice MSK a.s. (vazba:
  K. Hruška, statutární orgán) [registr smluv]"*, *"pá 10. 7. Dar 350 tis. Kč pro ANO 2011 od Agrofond s.r.o. —
  překryv vlastníků 38 % [hlídač státu /sponzoring]"*.

Every one of those carries a provenance label — `zdroj: psp.cz — jmenovitá hlasování`, `zdroj: registr smluv ⋈ ares`.
**The provenance chrome the loops optimized is the exact mechanism that makes the fabricated data look
authoritative.** A citizen cannot distinguish the invented Karel Hruška from the real Karel Haas; both are
presented in the same typography with the same citation footer.

Two of the five modules the nav advertises are the same story end to end:

- **`/hlasovani` (VoteTrack)** — five invented votes (*"14. 7. 2026 · sn. tisk 486 přijato Novela zákona o zadávání
  veřejných zakázek 108 : 62"*), footered *"vzorek 5 z 5 214 hlasování 9. období · psp.cz — denní ingesce."*
  There is no ingestion. There are no 5 214 votes.
- **`/rozpocty` (BudgetMirror)** — invented municipal finances for Beroun, Písek, Třebíč, Krnov, Kolín, Cheb
  (*"dluh na obyvatele 12 600 Kč"*), footered *"MONITOR, čtvrtletně."*

And the demo does not merely mislead — **it is broken**. `DashboardPage.tsx:178` maps the mock MPs to
`/poslanec/${m.id}` where `id` is a string slug (`"novakova-p"`), while `app/poslanec/[id]/page.tsx:36` does
`Number(id)` → `notFound()`. I verified all five:

```
/poslanec/novakova-p   404
/poslanec/pokorna-e    404
```

**The primary call-to-action on the app's control room is five dead links to five people who do not exist.** That
is the first click a new reader makes. Nothing further in this audit costs a reader more than this.

## Part 1 — Top 10 readability/UX defects, ranked by cost to the reader

### 1. The demo layer is indistinguishable from the real product, and its links 404
**Files:** `lib/civic/data.ts` (whole file), `app/page.tsx`, `app/dashboard/page.tsx`,
`features/dashboard/DashboardPage.tsx:32,54,178`, `features/landing/LandingPage.tsx:14`, `app/hlasovani/`, `app/rozpocty/`
**Rendered:** "Petra Nováková Piráti · Praha 2 88,3", "Agrofond s.r.o. IČO 470 12 336", "vzorek 5 z 5 214 hlasování"
**Cost:** total. Once a reader discovers one invented MP, every real number in the app becomes unverifiable to
them. This is the difference between "an app with rough edges" and "an app I don't believe."
**Smallest fix — delete:** remove `/hlasovani` and `/rozpocty` from `features/shell/navModel.ts:39,48`; delete
`lib/civic/data.ts` and point the landing's `Standings`/`LiveSpecimen` and the dashboard's leaderboard at
`getLeaderboardData()`, which already returns the real 207 with real pspIds. The real top-5 (Haas 96,8, Vesecká
95,4, Malá 95,4…) is *more* compelling than the invented one and the links would work. Ship three honest modules,
not five, two of which are props.

### 2. The persistent sidebar publishes numbers that contradict every page it sits on
**Files:** `messages/cs.json:95,102,109,116,123` (+ `en.json`), duplicated at `lib/civic/data.ts:153-197`;
rendered by `features/shell/Sidebar.tsx:64-66` via `sidebarParts.tsx:34-35`
**Rendered on every single page:**

| Sidebar says | The page says |
|---|---|
| `CivicScore … 200` | *"Všech 207 poslanců"* |
| `FollowTheMoney Síť peněz 2,1 mld Kč` | *"veřejné peníze v dosahu 18,7 mld. Kč"* |
| `LawWatch Monitor legislativy 312` | *"141 sněmovních tisků / 288 novelizovaných zákonů"* |
| `VoteTrack … 5 214` | nothing real exists |
| `BudgetMirror … 6 254` | nothing real exists |

These are **translation-catalog string literals**. They are copy, not data. They can never become correct, and
they are the one element visible in the reader's peripheral vision on 100% of pageviews — permanently asserting a
9× understatement of the money figure directly beside the real one.
**Smallest fix — delete:** drop the `metricValue`/`metricLabel` keys from all five module entries in both
catalogs and delete the `{labels.metric(entry)}` span at `Sidebar.tsx:64-68`. A nav rail does not need numbers.

### 3. `/penize`'s headline number and default sort are the thing the page itself warns you not to read
**Files:** `features/money/FollowTheMoneyPage.tsx:38-43` (tile), `features/money/getMoneyData.ts:41,73-76,100`,
`features/money/components/TiesLedger.tsx:80-81` (`useState<SortKey>("reach")`, dir `-1`)
**Rendered:** headline *"veřejné peníze v dosahu — 18,7 mld. Kč"*; row 1 of the ledger:

> **Petr Hladík · KDU-ČSL · ARENA BRNO, a.s. IČO 09133267 · dozorčí / správní · čeká na kontrolu · ukončeno 2022 · 6,1 mld. Kč**

An **ended, non-executive supervisory-board seat at a municipal arena company** is presented as the single largest
item in Czech politics, at one third of the app's headline figure. Rows 2–9 are the same shape: regional
hospitals, waterworks, municipal transit companies. Meanwhile the page's own `TieClassExplainer` says, in Czech,
*"Velké číslo u stewarda proto NIKDY nečtěte jako u vlastníka."*

**The page sorts by a quantity, then tells you the quantity is meaningless.** No reader obeys a footnote over a
sort order. The disclaimer *"nikdy jako obvinění"* does not survive contact with a table ranked by alleged money.
**Smallest fix:** change the default `sortKey` to tie class (`vlastník / jednatel` first, `dozorčí / správní`
last), and demote reach from a headline tile to an ordinary column. Replace the headline tile with the number
that *is* a finding: **how many MPs have an owner-class tie to a firm taking public money.**

### 4. The case file buries its own story under five items it tells you to ignore
**Files:** `features/money/getMpDetail.ts:40`, `features/money/getMoneyData.ts:84` (comment: *"strongest evidence
first within a case file"*), `features/money/MpCaseFilePage.tsx:77-90`
**Rendered:** `/penize/6786` (Róbert Teleky). Cards 1–5 are hospital supervisory boards, all ended 2021–2022, all
flagged by the app itself as steward-class. **Card 6 of 6** is:

> **Teleky Medicus s.r.o. · Jednatel · vlastník / jednatel · trvá · úplný trojúhelník** — *"Jednatel + 50%
> společník, aktivní; rodinné s.r.o. (Róbert + Dana Teleková 50/50). Úplný trojúhelník (zakázky+dotace+dar)."*

That is the story: an active family company, majority-owned by the sitting MP, that takes public contracts, takes
subsidies, **and donates to his party**. It is the only tie on the page the app's own taxonomy calls a real
FollowTheMoney link. It is last, because it is smallest — 11 mil. Kč against a hospital's 437 mil.

The stat strip compounds it: **"zakázky 65,4 mil. Kč / dotace 592,0 mil. Kč / dary straně 10 tis. Kč"** sums all
six ties, so Teleky is introduced as a 592-million-crown man when his own firm's subsidy is 20 tis. Kč.
**Smallest fix:** sort ties by class first, money second — one comparator change at `getMpDetail.ts:40`. Scope the
stat strip to owner-class ties, or split it into two strips ("vlastní firmy" / "veřejné instituce, kde byl v
dozoru").

### 5. `/penize/kauzy` renders the internal review-queue record to citizens
**File:** `features/money/KauzyPage.tsx:158-171` — a generic `Object.entries(d.proposedAnnotation).map(...)` with
`JSON.stringify(v)` at L167
**Rendered verbatim on a public page:**

```
navržená anotace (nezapsáno)
type : media_lead_annotation
targetNode : psp:person:6463 (Aleš Juchelka)
requiresGate : true
edgeProposed : false
edgeReason : No ARES VR record identifies Aleš Juchelka as an officer…
proposedFields : {"frontier_note":"Q-money-6 verified pass (batch-005, Opus-corrected 2026-07-25): …"}
```

Because the two payloads carry different key sets, the block is not even consistent between the two cases. Worse,
the *prose* leaks the machinery: *"Prior batch-002 **Sonnet** review of this same ARES-VR record…"*,
*"**Opus-verified** 2026-07-25"*, *"this pass did NOT independently locate…"*. **The model names are on the public
page of a transparency project.** A reader learns that a chatbot graded a politician.
**Smallest fix — delete:** remove the entire `navrzená anotace` block (L158-171). It is a gate artifact; the gate
is `/admin`'s job. Nothing is lost to the reader.

### 6. Both money dossiers and all 44 collision findings are in English
**Files:** `features/money/getLeadDossiers.ts:18` (payloads are English JSON), `/zakony/kolize` findings
**Measured:** `/penize/kauzy` — 2 of 2 dossiers, ~3 000 words, 100% English. `/zakony/kolize` — 44 paragraphs
opening *"Both bills issue a literal search-and-replace on the identical source string…"*. The CS/EN toggle in the
header does nothing to any of it.
**Cost:** the app's two most valuable artifacts are unreadable to the audience they are about. A Czech reader
arriving at the parental-allowance collision hits: *"a textbook 'search-and-replace on the same substring, two
different replacement values' collision."*
**Smallest fix:** this is content, not code — but the *structural* fix is to stop rendering `whatSourcesSustain` /
finding prose raw. Give each finding a one-sentence Czech `headline` field and render **only that** by default,
with the English evidence prose behind "plné odůvodnění" (a toggle that already exists on kolize).

### 7. The case file leaks graph-reconciliation apologetics into every card
**Files:** `features/money/MpCaseFilePage.tsx:183-185` (`tie.flags.map(fl => <Flag>{fl}</Flag>)` — raw machine
tokens, untranslated), `:199-206` (`poznámka rekonciliace`), `:217-219` (`zdroj: {tie.source}`)
**Rendered, twice per card, six cards on `/penize/6786`:**

```
stale-ongoing-in-graph
poznámka rekonciliace : ARES VR: člen dozorčí rady 2014-09-26→2021-03-24 · peníze:
  historical-no-money · graf tvrdil „ongoing" — nepřesné
zdroj : hlidac:osoby/robert-teleky · udalosti[Soukromá pracovní] ·
  "Krajská nemocnice T. Bati, a. s."→IČO 27661989 · 2014-01-01–ongoing
```

This is **the app apologising to itself about its own pipeline, in front of the reader.** `stale-ongoing-in-graph`
is a raw string written by `scripts/case-loops/money/reconcile-ares-vr.ts:375` and rendered untranslated. *"graf
tvrdil 'ongoing' — nepřesné"* tells a citizen that the graph was wrong — which is honest engineering and useless
journalism. The reader does not know what "the graph" is.
**Smallest fix — delete:** drop the `zdroj :` line (L217-219) and the raw `tie.flags` map (L183-185); keep only
the human-readable date range that `poznámka rekonciliace` already contains, rendered as plain text: *"člen
dozorčí rady 2014–2021 (ukončeno)"*. Keep the six registry deep-links — those are excellent and are the only
provenance a reader can actually act on.

### 8. `/zebricek` cannot answer "why is this MP ranked here", which is its only job
**Files:** `features/civicscore/components/LeaderboardTable.tsx:235` (`rows.map` — no slice, no pagination, no
virtualization), `MiniBreakdown` six-segment bar per row
**Measured:** 207 rows rendered at once → **1 045 363 bytes**, **645 `<svg>`**, **1 533 `<path>`**, 223 buttons,
738 ms TTFB. And the bars do not discriminate: the top ~50 MPs are all `Práce ve výborech 20/20`, `Legislativní
výstup 20/20`, `Vystoupení v sále 15/15`. Haas (96,8) beats Vesecká (95,4) **entirely on Docházka, 8 vs 7** — a
1-point difference inside a 10-point segment roughly four pixels wide. 1 533 vector paths are spent rendering six
near-identical bars 207 times, and the one number that explains the ranking is invisible.
**Smallest fix — delete:** remove `MiniBreakdown` from the list rows entirely and print the **single component
where this MP most deviates from the chamber median** as text ("+9 účast", "−2 docházka"). Keep the full
six-segment breakdown on the profile, where there is room for it and it already exists.

### 9. Every dossier is cut at character 240, mid-word, exactly before the payoff
**File:** `features/profile/components/ExpandableText.tsx:17` — `const COLLAPSE_AT = 240;`, L28
`text.slice(0, COLLAPSE_AT)` — a hard character slice with no word or sentence boundary
**Rendered** (Radim Fiala, `Poznámky k datům`):

> *"Peněžní vazba: mezi šesti propojenými firmami jsou IF Holding a.s. (IČO 02867681) a IF FACILITY a.s. (IČO
> 27720152, dříve IF KINGS SECURITY s.r.o. z Pro… **více**"*

The dossiers are the best thing in this app. Fiala's contains a real finding — SPD deputy chairman, chairs the
Economic Committee that reviews energy legislation, and reportedly became chair of ČEZ's supervisory board. That
sentence, and the money-tie sentence above, are always past character 240, because the first 240 characters are
always the setup. Compounding it: the section carrying the journalism is titled **"Poznámky k datům"** (data
footnotes) and placed **last**, below a "Legislativní stopa" that is mostly procedural dates.
**Smallest fix:** cut at the first sentence boundary after 240 rather than mid-word (a 2-line change), and swap
the order of `Legislativní stopa` and `Poznámky k datům` in `DossierSection.tsx` so the finding is above the
procedure. Better: rename `Poznámky k datům` → `Co jsme ověřili`.

### 10. Inline audit caveats interrupt the prose a citizen is reading
**File:** `features/profile/components/DossierSection.tsx:64` (`publicRole` rendered raw)
**Rendered** (Radim Fiala, "Veřejná role", mid-sentence):

> *"...Podle zpravodajství (iROZHLAS, červen 2026 — **citační URL chybí v tomto dossieru, NEOVĚŘENO touto
> verifikací**) se navíc stal předsedou dozorčí rady polostátní společnosti ČEZ."*

The single most newsworthy clause in the entire application is wrapped in a shouted parenthetical about the
dossier's own citation hygiene. Same pattern on `/zakony/kolize`, which tells the reader to go read a file in the
git repo: *"viz **docs/data-analysis/case-law/handoff.md**"*, and *"13 × nález z dávky 5 pochází z přeregenerované
topologie (150 → 567 hran 'amends'), kterou orchestrátor zatím neschválil pro živý graf."*
**Smallest fix:** move verification status out of the sentence and into the existing per-item confidence badge —
the UI already has `signál 4 · spolehlivost medium`. Prose says what happened; a badge says how sure we are.
Delete the repo path from public copy.

**Runner-up (11):** `TiesLedger.tsx:223` prints **"211 z 211 vazeb"** directly above a table showing 25 rows
(`PAGE_SIZE = 25`, L46). Reader counts 25 and distrusts the 211.

## Part 2 — Scale verdict per surface

| Surface | Population | Payload | State | At 10× |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/` landing | 5 fake MPs | 106 KB | **Broken at n=1** — fixed-size fiction | never becomes true |
| `/dashboard` | 5 fake MPs | 113 KB | **Broken at n=1** — 5/5 links 404 | never becomes true |
| `/hlasovani` | 5 of "5 214" | 121 KB | **Broken at n=1** — fabricated | never becomes true |
| `/rozpocty` | 6 of "6 254" | 80 KB | **Broken at n=1** — fabricated | never becomes true |
| `/zebricek` | 207 MPs | **1 045 KB**, 1 533 paths, 738 ms | **Already broken** | 10 MB — unusable |
| `/poslanec/[id]` | 1 of 207 | 100 KB | **Healthy** — best surface; fixed cost/page, `generateStaticParams` pre-renders all 207 | scales fine |
| `/penize` | 211 ties | 403 KB | **Strained** — renders fine (paginates at 25), but serializes all 211 to ship 25 | 4 MB for 25 visible rows |
| `/penize/[id]` | 6 ties | 137 KB | **Already broken as reading** — the story is card 6 of 6 | at 60 ties, unfindable |
| `/penize/kauzy` | 2 dossiers | 134 KB | **Breaks at n=3** — `DOSSIER_FILES` is a hardcoded 2-element array reading from `docs/`; a third case requires a code change and a deploy | structurally impossible |
| `/zakony` | 141 bills | **922 KB**, 6 537 B/bill | **Strained** — no pagination | 9 MB |
| `/zakony/kolize` | 36 pairs | 350 KB, 44 English paragraphs, one page | **Already broken as reading** | 3.5 MB wall |

**The pattern:** nothing that renders *one* thing is broken. Everything that renders *all* things is. The app has
no concept of a summary layer — every list page ships the detail page's payload for every row. `/zebricek`
serializes each MP's full `trend` object (6 components × prior/current/delta), `counts`, `provenance.pass`, and
`effortPublicRole` prose — **5 050 bytes per MP, none of it rendered in the list** — for all 207.

**What breaks first at 10×:** `/zebricek` (already over), then `/zakony`. **What is safe:** `/poslanec/[id]`.
**What cannot scale at any multiple:** `/penize/kauzy`, because the content store is a hardcoded filename array
in a `docs/` folder.

## Part 3 — Three things to delete outright

**1. `lib/civic/data.ts` and everything it feeds.**
The mock module (landing specimen, dashboard graph + activity feed + leaderboard, `/hlasovani`, `/rozpocty`, and
the duplicated `MODULES` metrics) plus the `metricValue` keys at `messages/cs.json:95-123`. This is ~40% of the
app's rendered surface and 100% of its fabricated surface. It is the only thing here that damages the project's
credibility rather than merely its usability, and deleting it costs zero real information. Three honest modules
beat five, two of which are props with dead links.

**2. Every trace of the pipeline's conversation with itself.**
Specifically: the `navrzená anotace (nezapsáno)` block (`KauzyPage.tsx:158-171`), the raw `tie.flags` tokens
(`MpCaseFilePage.tsx:183-185` — `stale-ongoing-in-graph`, `historical-no-money`), the `zdroj : hlidac:osoby/…`
lineage line (`:217-219`), the `graf tvrdil „ongoing" — nepřesné` reconciliation apologetics, every `pass N` /
`dávka N` / `batch-005` / `P52` / `Q-money-5` label, the model names (`Sonnet`, `Opus-verified`), and the repo
path `docs/data-analysis/case-law/handoff.md` printed on a public page. None of this is provenance — provenance is
the six registry deep-links on each tie card, which are genuinely good and should stay. This is **process
narration**, and the loops produced it because they were graded on auditability rather than on comprehension.

**3. The "veřejné peníze v dosahu" headline and the reach-descending sort.**
The number 18,7 mld. Kč is not a finding; it is the sum of the turnover of every public institution any MP has
ever formally supervised. The app knows this — `TieClassExplainer` says so in Czech. A number your own
documentation instructs the reader to discount does not belong in 48-point type at the top of the page, and it
certainly must not be the sort key of the table beneath it. Delete the tile, change the comparator
(`TiesLedger.tsx:80`, `getMpDetail.ts:40`). Also delete the *"čeká na kontrolu"* badge from the ledger's rows:
211 of 211 rows carry it, 0 are verified, so it conveys exactly zero bits per row while consuming a column. Say
it once, in the section header, where it already is said.

## Part 4 — Would a journalist find a story tomorrow?

Yes — there are at least two real ones — and no, they would not find them. The best story in this application is
on `/zakony/kolize`: **four bills currently before the Chamber (tisky 4, 112, 121, 198) each perform a literal
search-and-replace on the same string, "350 000 Kč", in §30 of the parental-allowance law, and each replaces it
with a different number — 420 000, 370 000, 400 000 and 400 000.** Whichever passes second silently overwrites the
first or fails to find its target text. That is a genuine, verifiable, front-page legislative-process failure that
no Czech newsroom has, and it is *twelve screens down a 350 KB page*, written in English, beginning *"Both bills
issue a literal search-and-replace on the identical source string,"* and footered *"dávka 5 · deterministic
partitioned pre-check on the REGENERATED (not-yet-live) amends topology, ranked by a money/coefficient-literal
signal (P52), LLM close-read."* The second story — Radim Fiala chairing the Economic Committee while reportedly
chairing ČEZ's supervisory board — is on his profile, in the section labelled "data footnotes", cut off at
character 240. But the journalist never reaches either, because they land on a home page starring a Pirate MP
named Petra Nováková who does not exist, click the top row of the control room and get a 404, then open
FollowTheMoney and are told the largest money story in Czech politics is a KDU-ČSL deputy's supervisory seat at a
Brno arena that ended four years ago. They close the tab at that point, and they are right to. **The app's problem
is not that it lacks stories. It is that it ranks every page by volume — money, rows, provenance, passes — and a
story is never the biggest thing on a page.**
